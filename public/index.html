    Hearing the room

    Sonos gives apps no access to its microphones. To hear the room, open this address on a phone or laptop near the speaker and choose Room mic:

    Room → station

    Leave this device near the Sonos speaker with the screen on. It sends the room's sound to the station; the station's voice comes out of the Sonos speaker.
